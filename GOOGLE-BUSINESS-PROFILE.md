# Google Business Profile — setup pack for Jagdambey Departmental Store

Create it free at **google.com/business** (or the "Google Maps" app → Contribute → Add place).

This matters **more than the website** for people searching in Sardulgarh. The website
supports it; the profile is what actually shows up in Maps and in "near me" searches.

---

## 1. Business name — read this first

**Enter exactly what is on your signboard:**

```
Jagdambey Departmental Store
```

**Do NOT** write `Jagdambey Departmental Store - Best Grocery Store in Sardulgarh` or
similar. Stuffing keywords into the name is against Google's rules. Competitors can
report it and Google **suspends the profile** — you lose the listing entirely, which is
far worse than ranking second.

### Getting "Ashok Kumar Bareta Wale" to work for you

There is no "alternate name" field. But Google *does* learn other names for a shop
from these places, and all of them are legitimate:

1. **The description** (below) — it is written in there.
2. **Your website** — already done. The site now declares
   `alternateName: "Ashok Kumar Bareta Wale"` in its structured data, and shows the
   name on the page in English, Hindi and Punjabi.
3. **Reviews** — when customers write "Ashok Kumar Bareta Wale de shop ton saman
   liya", Google links the name to you. This is the strongest signal.
4. **Your signboard** — if the board itself says it, add a photo of the board. If you
   ever repaint it, putting both names on the board makes this official.

## 3. Categories — this is the biggest ranking lever

Your **primary category** decides which searches you can appear in at all. Pick:

**Primary:** `Grocery store`

**Additional categories** (add all that apply — they are free and each one opens up
new searches):

- `General store`
- `Convenience store`
- `Variety store`
- `Stationery store`
- `Cosmetics store`
- `Gift shop`
- `Discount store`

Do not add a category for something you don't actually sell — it hurts relevance and
brings people who leave bad reviews.

## 4. Everything else to fill in

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Address** | Near Geeta Bhawan, Sardulgarh, Mansa, Punjab + your PIN code |
| **Pin location** | Drag the map pin **onto your shop**, not the road or the landmark |
| **Phone** | 94645 94106 (primary), 94635 04900 (additional) |
| **Website** | Your domain once you have one |
| **Hours** | Your real hours, all 7 days. Add **special hours** for Diwali, Lohri, Holi |
| **Service area** | Sardulgarh + the nearby villages you deliver to |
| **Opening date** | The year the shop opened — adds credibility |

**Attributes to switch on** (only the true ones):
Home delivery · In-store shopping · In-store pickup · Cash only? No — tick UPI /
Google Pay / mobile payments · Wheelchair accessible entrance (if true) ·
Small business · Identifies as family-owned

**Products section:** add your main items with photos and prices. Every product you
add is another thing Google can match a search against. Start with 15–20.

**Services section:** add `Mobile recharge`, `Home delivery`, `Festival gift packs`.

## 5. Photos — Google ranks profiles with more, fresher photos higher

Upload on day one:

- **Logo** — use `assets/brand/icon-512.png` from your website folder
- **Cover photo** — the shopfront, **shutter up**, daytime, signboard readable
- **Exterior** — 3 photos from different angles, including the signboard close up
- **Interior** — 5+ photos of the aisles, counter, pooja section, stationery rack
- **Products** — 10+ photos of actual shelves
- **At work** — you or your staff serving a customer

Then add **2–3 new photos every week**. This is one of the few things you fully
control that Google measures.

## 6. Reviews — the single biggest thing after category and distance

In a town the size of Sardulgarh, whoever has the most genuine reviews usually wins.
Competitors here likely have very few.

**How to get them:**
1. In your profile, copy the **"Ask for reviews"** short link.
2. Turn it into a **QR code** (free at any QR site), print it, stick it at the counter
   and on your delivery bags.
3. Ask every satisfied customer face to face: *"Google te review kar dena ji."*
   Asking in person works far better than a message.
4. Aim for **10 reviews in the first month**, then 5–10 a month steadily.

**Reply to every single review**, good or bad, within a day. Google counts this.
In your reply, naturally use words people search for:
*"Thank you ji. Aap ko pooja samagri aur grocery hamesha taaza milegi — Jagdambey
Departmental Store, Geeta Bhawan ke paas."*

**Never buy reviews.** Google detects bought reviews and removes the profile.

## 7. Posts — free advertising, most shops never use it

Post once a week from the profile:
- Festival stock arrivals (Diwali, Lohri, Holi, Rakhi, Karva Chauth)
- New items
- Offers
- "Home delivery available today"

Posts appear directly in your Maps listing and keep the profile "active", which helps.

Write the **Name, Address and Phone exactly the same** on every site:

## 10. Honest answer about ranking first

Google Maps ranks on three things:

1. **Relevance** — how well your categories, description and products match the search.
   *You control this fully.*
2. **Distance** — how close you are to the person searching.
   *You cannot control this.* Someone standing at the other end of town may see a
   shop nearer to them first, no matter what you do.
3. **Prominence** — reviews, ratings, photos, posts, website, mentions elsewhere.
   *You control most of this.*

So nobody can promise the #1 spot for every search. But **for a town like Sardulgarh
this is very winnable** — the competition mostly has incomplete profiles and few
reviews. Do points 1–9 above properly and you should be at or near the top for
searches like *"departmental store Sardulgarh"*, *"kirana store near me"*,
*"grocery shop Sardulgarh"* and *"pooja saman Sardulgarh"* within 2–3 months.
