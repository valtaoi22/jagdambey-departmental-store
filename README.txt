Jagdambey Departmental Store — website
======================================

Open index.html in a browser to view the site.

TO EDIT YOUR DETAILS, PRICES AND HOURS:
  Open  assets/js/store-data.js  in Notepad and change the values.
  Full instructions are in EDIT-GUIDE.md

Lines marked  ⚠️ CONFIRM  in store-data.js are guessed values.
Please check them before the site goes live.

The site works in English, हिंदी and ਪੰਜਾਬੀ (buttons in the header).

TO PUT IT ONLINE (FREE):
  Open  HOSTING.md  — drag-and-drop hosting in about 2 minutes.

FOR YOUR GOOGLE LISTING:
  Open  GOOGLE-BUSINESS-PROFILE.md  — ready-to-paste description,
  categories, photo checklist and how to rank in Sardulgarh.
